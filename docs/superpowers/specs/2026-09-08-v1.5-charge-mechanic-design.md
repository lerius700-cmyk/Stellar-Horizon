# v1.5 — Charge Mechanic + 4 New Laser Archetypes + Procedural Impact

**Date:** 2026-09-08
**Status:** Approved
**Author:** Lerius + Mavis
**Target release:** v1.5.0 (post-v1.4.0)

## Overview

v1.4.0 (released 2026-08-31) shipped 5 laser archetypes (yellow plasma, red pulse, blue ion, green acid, purple void) on a 6-frame sprite sheet, with 4 of 10 weapons aliased to existing archetypes (no distinct sheets for weapons 5–8).

v1.5 expands the system in three coordinated ways:

1. **4 new laser archetypes** (cyan ice, orange flame, white lightning, magenta heart) — each gets its own 6-frame sheet at the standard 29×7 size.
2. **Charge mechanic** — 4 of the 10 weapons (5, 6, 7, 8) gain a "hold fire" interaction. Each weapon's charge has a distinct behavior (continuous beam, single big shot, piercing stream, boomerang). Weapons 0–4 and 9 are tap-only (no change).
3. **Procedural impact** — every bullet/beam that hits an enemy or the screen edge spawns a short-lived impact burst (particles + halo) at the contact point, so the player can see "this hit something" instead of bullets silently disappearing.

### Generation pipeline (canonical)

1. **AI gen** — `connector__matrix__generate_image` with `aspect_ratio: 21:9` and a prompt that follows the laser_06 pattern: *"Each projectile is a VERTICAL sprite: a solid angular [HEAD] at the BOTTOM and a translucent [TRAIL] extending FAR UPWARD. ... The 6 sprites must be in a single HORIZONTAL ROW."* This pattern lets the postprocess rotate 90° CCW to produce horizontal sprites with the head on the right.
2. **`regen_laser_strip_one.py`** — finds colored content bbox (excluding the AI watermark and white margin), splits into 6 vertical slices, rotates each 90° CCW, resizes to 29 wide × variable height, fits to 29×7 with center-crop, concatenates to 174×7.
3. **`regen_laser_strip_six.py`** — right-aligns each frame so the focus/head is on the right edge and the trail extends to the left. Also strips AI-watermark gray pixels via `clean_gray_pixels`.

Output: `laser_NN_sheet.png` (174×7, 6 frames of 29×7) and `laser_NN.png` (29×7, first-frame reference for HUD icons).

|---|---|---|---|---|

| 4 purple void | laser_05 | Normal shot | (same) | — |
| **5 orange fireball** | laser_07 | Normal fireball | **Continuous beam** (lanzallamas) | Beam ends |
| **6 white piercing** | laser_08 | Normal bolt | Charge bar fills | **1.2s** held → release = single big Megaman-style bolt (3× damage) |

| 9 rainbow streak | laser_05 | Normal shot | (same) | — |

### Hold vs tap detection

Player.update() needs three states on the fire input (currently just one bool `firing`):
- `fire_pressed_this_frame: bool` — true on the frame the key transitions from up to down. Triggers a normal shot on tap.
- `fire_held: bool` — true while the key is down. Used by the charge bar visualization.
- `fire_released_this_frame: bool` — true on the frame the key transitions from down to up. Triggers the charged shot for weapons 6 and 7.

Implementation: pass `events: list[pygame.event.Event]` to `Player.update()` (in addition to `keys`), or extend the scene to call `player.on_fire_pressed()` / `player.on_fire_released()` on KEYDOWN/KEYUP. The cleanest path: add explicit `pressed_this_frame` and `released_this_frame` booleans on the player, set by the scene's event loop.

### Charge state (per weapon, only relevant for 5/6/7/8)

- `charge_time: float` — seconds the fire button has been held (0 if not charging).
- `charge_complete: bool` — true once `charge_time >= CHARGE_TIME_FOR_WEAPON[weapon]`.
- For weapons 6, 7: `CHARGE_TIME_FOR_WEAPON[weapon]` is 1.2s / 1.5s.
- For weapons 5, 8: charge is "infinite" (continuous emission while held). `charge_complete` is true after 0.3s (used to fade in the visual aura).

### Charged shot types (subclasses of PlayerBullet)

```python
class ChargedMegamanBolt(PlayerBullet):   # weapon 6
    DAMAGE = 3
    SIZE = (32, 8)        # bigger than normal
    POOL_SIZE = 4

class ChargedBoomerangHeart(PlayerBullet): # weapon 7
    DAMAGE = 2
    SIZE = (16, 16)
    POOL_SIZE = 4
    LIFETIME_S = 1.5      # returns after 0.6s, dies at 1.5s
    RETURN_VELOCITY = -1  # sign flip

class ChargedPiercingCrystal(PlayerBullet): # weapon 8
    DAMAGE = 2
    SIZE = (24, 8)
    POOL_SIZE = 8
    PIERCING = True       # doesn't collide with first enemy hit
    POOL_SPAWN_INTERVAL_S = 1.5
```

These share the `PlayerBullet` interface (`.spawn(x, y, vx, vy, weapon, spawn_time)`, `.update(dt)`, `.hitbox()`) so the collision handler doesn't need changes.

### Beam entity (weapon 5 only, separate from bullets)

```python
class Beam:
    """Continuous flamethrower beam from player to impact point.
    
    One active beam at a time (the player can only hold one weapon).
    Drawn procedurally in fx/beam_renderer.py — body has variable
    width (random per frame for natural flame motion), color gradient
    from orange (origin) to white-yellow (impact). Emits dispersion
    particles at the impact point every frame.
    """
    start: tuple[float, float]   # player muzzle (x + offset, y)
    end: tuple[float, float]     # impact or max range
    width_base: int = 8
    length_max: int = 320
    damage_per_tick: int = 1
    tick_interval_s: float = 0.05
    alive: bool = True
```

Beam tick (every `tick_interval_s`): deal damage to all enemies within `Beam.hitbox_strip()` (a thin rectangle from start to end), emit 3–5 particles at the impact point with random scatter.

## 3. Ship charge UI (Megaman-style aura on the ship)

The ship gains a "charging aura" overlay while the fire button is held. Per-weapon color, growing radius, pulsing at full charge.

### Render layer

`fx/ship_charge_aura.py` — a render-only module (no state, just `draw(surface, player, weapon, charge_time)`).

Visual:
- 2 concentric circles centered on the ship
- Outer ring: weapon color at 30% alpha, radius 8 + `charge_time * 12` (caps at 32)
- Inner disc: weapon color at 50% alpha, radius 4 + `charge_time * 4` (caps at 16)
- At `charge_complete` (for weapons 6, 7, 8): pulse alpha with sin(t*8) — visible "ready" feedback
- For weapon 5 (beam): once `charge_time > 0`, the aura stays at full size and connects to the beam visually (the beam's start point is the aura's center)

|---|---|

| 6 | `#FFFFFF` (white) |
| 7 | `#FF44AA` (magenta) |

Drawn with `pygame.draw.circle` + alpha composite (pygame.gfxdraw for AA on the inner disc). Renders BETWEEN the player sprite and the bullet/beam layers.

## 4. Procedural impact burst

When any player bullet OR beam hitbox collides with an enemy, boss, or screen edge, spawn a one-shot impact burst at the contact point.

### ImpactBurst system

```python
class ImpactBurst:
    """One-shot particle burst at an impact point.
    
    Pool of 16 reusable bursts (only a few on-screen at once).
    Each burst has a lifetime of 0.3s, fades alpha 255→0.
    Emits particles in a forward cone (toward the bullet's
    velocity direction) plus 1-2 random sparks.
    """
    x, y: float
    vx_dir, vy_dir: float   # bullet's direction (for cone)
    lifetime: float
    max_lifetime: float
    alive: bool
    particles: list  # references to engine particles
```

### WEAPON_IMPACT_PARAMS (per weapon)

```python
WEAPON_IMPACT_PARAMS = (

    # 1 red pulse — red sparks, medium
    WeaponImpact(particle_kind=P_SPARK, color=(255, 100, 100), count=10, spread_deg=25, speed_px_s=140),
    # 2 blue ion — blue sparks, small
    WeaponImpact(particle_kind=P_SPARK, color=(120, 200, 255), count=6, spread_deg=15, speed_px_s=100),
    # 3 green acid — green dust, large
    WeaponImpact(particle_kind=P_DUST, color=(80, 255, 120), count=14, spread_deg=30, speed_px_s=80),
    # 4 purple void — purple glow, large
    WeaponImpact(particle_kind=P_GLOW, color=(180, 80, 255), count=10, spread_deg=35, speed_px_s=90),
    # 5 orange fire — fire particles, large
    WeaponImpact(particle_kind=P_FIRE, color=(255, 140, 40), count=12, spread_deg=40, speed_px_s=110),
    # 6 white lightning — white sparks + crackle
    WeaponImpact(particle_kind=P_SPARK, color=(255, 255, 255), count=14, spread_deg=20, speed_px_s=200),
    # 7 magenta heart — pink glow + hearts
    WeaponImpact(particle_kind=P_GLOW, color=(255, 100, 180), count=12, spread_deg=30, speed_px_s=100),
    # 8 cyan ice — cyan ice shards
    WeaponImpact(particle_kind=P_SPARK, color=(140, 220, 255), count=10, spread_deg=25, speed_px_s=130),

    WeaponImpact(particle_kind=P_SPARK, color=(255, 200, 255), count=10, spread_deg=20, speed_px_s=150),

```

### Pool + hook

- `ImpactBurst` is owned by `GameplayScene`, with `IMPACT_POOL_SIZE = 16` (most bursts are short-lived; 16 covers ~6 simultaneous on-screen).
- The collision handler in `gameplay.py` calls `scene.spawn_impact(x, y, weapon, vx_dir, vy_dir)` after applying damage.
