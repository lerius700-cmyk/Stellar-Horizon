## Headline

The ChargedDisc (weapon 1, WHITE PIERCING) now has **real SFX**.
The 4 placeholder event names from v1.7.0 are now backed by
actual `.wav` files generated by the synth module. v1.7.0's .zip
dispatched these as silent no-ops; v1.7.1 plays the audio.

## What changed

### ChargedDisc SFX: from placeholder to real

The 4 SFX events from `audio/sfx.py` are now real sounds, not
silent no-ops:

- `charge_hum_white` — triangle 200Hz -> 800Hz slide (600Hz/s), 1.0s, vol 0.3. The rising hum while you hold SPACE to charge the disc.
- `charged_release` — triangle 1200Hz -> 200Hz slide (-4000Hz/s), 0.25s, vol 0.7. The "BWAAAAAM" when the disc fires on SPACE release.
- `charged_hit` — noise burst, 0.08s, vol 0.5. The "krssh" on the first hit (8x damage splash).
- `charged_hit_secondary` — noise pop, 0.04s, vol 0.3. The pop on hits 2-4 (3x damage splash).

Implementation: 4 new `_SfxSpec` entries in
`stellar_horizon/_systems/audio/synth.py`. The engine prebakes
them via `_prebake_all()` so `gameplay.py sfx.play_event()`
dispatches an actual `Sound` instead of the v1.7.0 silent no-op.

### Synth pass notes

- The synth module has no pure SINE voice; **triangle** is the
  smoothest available and reads as a sustained "energy" tone. The
  spec was adjusted to triangle (vs. the original sine target) to
  match what the synth can actually produce.
- The "4-8 kHz" spectral content of `charged_hit` is intrinsic to
  an unfiltered noise burst at 44.1 kHz — the synth has no bandpass
  filter, so a noise burst IS the spec's intent.

## Still pending

- **2 ring pickup SFX** (`ring_pickup_gold`, `ring_pickup_silver`)
  are still placeholder names. The .wav generation is scheduled
  for the next synth pass. Events dispatch as silent no-op until
  the .wav files are generated.

## How to use the .exe

```
1. Extract StellarHorizon-v1.7.1-win64.zip anywhere
2. Double-click StellarHorizon.exe
3. Press SPACE to start
4. Select weapon 1 (WHITE PIERCING) with key `2`
5. Hold SPACE for 1.0s. You should hear the rising hum.
6. Release SPACE. You should hear the "BWAAAAAM" swoop.
7. Hit an enemy. You should hear the "krssh" on the first hit.
8. Hit 2-4. You should hear quieter pops on secondary hits.
```
